Advocacy priorities

What MOW NSW is currently pushing for on members' behalf

Based on member consultation through 2026, MOW NSW's current advocacy priorities are:

  • Retaining CHSP as a distinct programme rather than folding it into Support at Home
  • Funding and reporting models that work for volunteer-run rural and regional services, not just large metro providers
  • Realistic implementation timelines for new reporting requirements, with lead time for smaller services without paid admin staff
  • Continued Financially Disadvantaged Fund access for services supporting low-income clients
